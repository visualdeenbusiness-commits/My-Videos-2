# Pip the Paper Rocket

**Length:** ~30 seconds · **Audience:** ages 3–7 · **Narration:** ~80 words (slow, warm read)

## Script

| Time | Visual | Narration |
|------|--------|-----------|
| 0:00–0:05 | A child's bedroom at night. A small paper rocket with crayon stars sits on the windowsill, staring at a big glowing moon. | "Pip was a little paper rocket who dreamed of reaching the moon." |
| 0:05–0:10 | Pip wiggles, hops, and flutters down onto the carpet. | "Every night, Pip tried to fly. Whoosh… flutter… flop!" |
| 0:10–0:16 | Pip looks sad. A girl in pajamas kneels and picks Pip up with a smile. | "'I'm just paper,' Pip sighed. But Mia picked Pip up and whispered, 'Let's try together!'" |
| 0:16–0:23 | Mia folds Pip's wings sharper, holds Pip high, and throws. Pip soars across the room and out the window. | "She folded Pip's wings strong and tall. One, two, three… WHOOSH!" |
| 0:23–0:30 | Pip glides up toward the moon, which smiles and winks. Mia waves from the window. | "Pip flew higher than ever before. And the moon smiled back. The end!" |

## Lesson

Big dreams get closer when friends help — and when you keep trying.

## Production notes

- **Style:** soft, crayon-and-paper textures; warm bedroom tones vs. cool blue night sky.
- **Sound:** gentle music box loop; paper rustle on "flutter… flop"; rising whoosh on the throw.
- **Voice:** friendly, unhurried; pause briefly after "flop!" and "three…".
